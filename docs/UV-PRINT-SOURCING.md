Research date: 2026-09-22. Supplier capabilities below are statements from official pages, not independently tested production results. No supplier was contacted and no order was placed.

## First recommendation

**Glowtronics is the strongest initial candidate for one-off fluorescent fabric.** Paper editions currently look more suitable for batch production through LamPro or Atmosphere. ## Implications for our print portal

A digital UV appearance and a physically reactive edition are separate products. Glowtronics cannot preserve the engine's per-mark choice between normal and reactive pigment. LamPro's spot channel is conceptually closer to a selective reveal, but its reaction color, size and batch constraints need confirmation. The screen preview is not a calibrated physical color proof.

Keep a future supplier-specific separation export: ordinary artwork plus a mask identifying reactive marks, rather than only a flattened image. The provider must confirm which files and inks it can actually use.
